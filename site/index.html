BayesTS Studio

Bayesian time series models,
from data to impulse responses.

A desktop application for VARs, VECs, dynamic factor models, FAVARs and unobserved components models that follows the bvartools workflow — data, model, priors, posterior draws and their application — without writing code. Every draw comes from BayesTS, a C++ library of Gibbs samplers.

Download for Windows Linux (snap) and other options

What it does

Build a model

The six Gaussian VARs and the six Gaussian VECs, with weakly exogenous variables, deterministic terms, BVS and SSVS variable selection, and the volatility break of Lenza and Primiceri (2022). Factor and unobserved components models are opened from a BayesTS model file.

Identify shocks

Impulse responses and variance decompositions, orthogonalised or generalised, or identified by sign restrictions and by sign and zero restrictions (Arias, Rubio-Ramírez and Waggoner 2018). Checked draw by draw against bvartools.

Decompose and connect

Historical decompositions, Diebold–Yilmaz spillovers, dynamic multipliers of exogenous variables, and inclusion probabilities.

Time-varying models

Coefficients, covariances, responses and decompositions at any period, chosen by its date, and a state variance explorer that shows what the setting does.

Factor models

For dynamic factor models and FAVARs: the factors' paths, the loadings, the share of each series the factors explain, and impulse responses of the whole panel — a FAVAR's identified as in Bernanke, Boivin and Eliasz (2005).

Trend and cycle

For unobserved components models: each series with its trend, and its cycle — the output gap of a GDP series — with bands over the sample.

Take the results with you

Every table as CSV, LaTeX, Markdown or to the clipboard; every figure as PDF, SVG or PNG.

Learn as you go

An optional assistant through a master thesis or a forecasting horse race, with worked examples and two papers reproduced step by step.

Linux

A snap, attached to every release from 0.2.0 on:

sudo snap install --dangerous ./bayests-studio_*.snap

Or build from source; the README has the steps.

Every release, with its notes and checksums, is on GitHub. Open source under the BSD 3-Clause License.
